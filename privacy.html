Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

MissNoCall is a service of WhiteFort Digital LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company ("MissNoCall", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect through missnocall.co and our services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • When you book a demo: your name, email, business name, phone number and website, entered in our booking form (run by Cal.com).
  • When you email us: the contents of your message and your contact details.
  • When we run an AI receptionist for your business: calls to your business number are answered by our system. That produces call recordings, transcripts, caller phone numbers, appointment details and messages. We process this information on behalf of your business, under our service agreement with you.

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

How we use it

  • To schedule and hold your demo, answer your questions and send you a quote.
  • To build, run, monitor and improve the AI receptionist for our clients, and to send them reports.
  • To bill our clients and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information.

Text messages (SMS)

When a business uses our service to send appointment reminders or follow-up texts, messages go only to people who have agreed to receive them. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving messages, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. This excludes service providers that deliver the messages for us.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the service, such as our voice-AI, telephony, calendar, email and hosting providers. They may use it only to provide services to us. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Health information

For medical practices that are covered by HIPAA, we sign a Business Associate Agreement and handle protected health information only as that agreement allows.

How long we keep it

We keep demo and email records for as long as needed to respond and follow up. We keep client call data for the length of our agreement, plus 30 days to allow export, unless the client asks for a shorter period or the law requires longer.

Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections and providers with recognized security programs. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing john@trymissnocall.com. If you called a business that uses MissNoCall, contact that business first — we will help them handle your request.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.

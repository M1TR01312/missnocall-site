Questions

What clinic owners ask us.

Will it sound like a robot?

No — it uses a natural, human-sounding voice. Book a demo and judge for yourself. If a caller asks, it's honest that it's an AI assistant.

Will it give medical advice?

No. It answers questions about treatments, prices and availability, and hands anything medical to your team.

Does it replace my receptionist?

No. It catches what your team can't — after hours, weekends, and calls that come in while they're busy.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Your existing number forwards to the AI when you want it to — for example, after hours or when the line is busy.

What about privacy?

Call data stays private to your business. For medical practices, we build on HIPAA-eligible platforms and sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA).

How much does it cost?

Plans start at $897 a month, plus a one-time setup. The exact price depends on your call volume, hours and what we connect to — you get it on the free demo, with no obligation.

What if we get more calls than the plan includes?

Every plan includes a set number of talk minutes: 500, 1,000 or 2,000 a month. We email you when you reach 80%, and any extra minutes are billed at a flat $0.60 per minute — never a surprise. If you're regularly over, we'll suggest the plan that fits.

How long does setup take?

Calls are answered within 3–7 business days of our kickoff call. Text-message features switch on once US carriers approve your number — usually 1–3 weeks.

Can I pay less?

Yes. With a 6- or 12-month plan your monthly fee and your setup both go down, and if you pay a year upfront setup is free. You can also save every month by referring other businesses. Your quote shows all savings in dollars.

How does billing work?

Half of the setup is due when you sign and the other half on the day your receptionist goes live. Your monthly plan starts on go-live and is charged automatically to your card or US bank account. Invoices come from WhiteFort Digital LLC, doing business as MissNoCall, and you can update your payment method or download invoices any time in our client billing portal.

Can I cancel?

Yes, with 30 days' notice by email. Setup is non-refundable. If you chose a 6- or 12-month plan and leave early, you pay back the savings you received, not the remaining months.
